2. FAST-RV

FAST-RV (First-order Automated Single-planet Tool for Radial Velocities) is a lightweight Python toolkit for taking a quick look at exoplanet radial velocities from VizieR. It estimates an orbital period, fits a simple Keplerian model, and plots the phase-folded RV curve. One planet, one instrument, and no waiting for an MCMC chain to reconsider its life choices.

It’s a first-pass tool for simple datasets. For a more detailed fit, I’d reach for RadVel or juliet.
